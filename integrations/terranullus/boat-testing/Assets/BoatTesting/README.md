# Boat testing

Open `Assets/Scenes/RowingBoatCrewTest.unity` and press Play for a ready-crewed rowing test: open water, two NPC rowers, and the player already in **Passenger aft**, with the forward passenger seat free. It uses the latest reusable boat prefab and its authored seat positions. **W** rows forward, **A/D** steer, **E** leaves/boards, **R** returns the player and crew to their seats on the moving boat, and **Escape** releases the cursor. The scenario supplies seating and recovery only; normal gameplay controls, rowing animations, IK and buoyancy run unchanged. There is no inspection-flight wrapper in this scene.

**Island > Boat Tests > Create Crewed Rowboat Test Scene** generates it from the current boat, Player and NPC prefabs without overwriting an existing scene. **Run Crewed Rowboat Scene Play Check** tests the saved scene directly, including initial crew/player seating, dynamic flotation, real W propulsion/animations and R recovery. It restores editing scenes and the prior Play start-scene setting; report `/tmp/crewed-rowboat-scene-play.txt`.

Open `Assets/Scenes/BoatTestbed.unity` on its own for a reusable sea-and-player template. It has no islands, seabed, terrain streaming or existing ships. The player starts in inspection flight so an empty testbed needs no platform.

Open `Assets/Scenes/RowingBoatTest.unity` for the first flotation and boarding test. It uses the existing Synty Polygon Pirates `SM_Veh_Boat_Rowing_01_Hull_Attachments` prefab, retaining its four oars. The player starts aboard. TheOne includes rowing poses and entry/exit transitions, with seat and oar IK. Crewed boats now use passenger commands for physical rowing and steering.

- **F** switches between inspection flight and normal character walking.
- **WASD** moves; **Q/E** descend/ascend in flight; **Shift** speeds up flight.
- **R** returns to the boat and restores walking, or resets to the airborne spawn in the empty template.
- **Escape** releases the cursor; click the game view to capture it again.

The scenes share `BoatTestEnvironment.asset` and `BoatTestWaves.asset`: fixed midday starting light, no clouds, and small ocean waves. Adjust these assets to test rougher conditions. They use the same ocean surface and buoyancy implementation as the main world. Open one test scene at a time, without OpenSeaWorld loaded alongside it, to keep a single global environment owner.

`RowingBoatTest.prefab` is a reusable wrapper around the vendor prefab; the vendor assets are untouched. A single dynamic body uses 32 transverse hull sections for displacement and water resistance. Hull Draft Loading calculates its total loading mass from Ocean Buoyancy > Draft and the displaced hull volume. The default vertical draft at the corrected bottom-of-hull resting angle is 0.45 m (about 1198 kg for this model), replacing the original lightly loaded 200 kg setup. Increasing Draft in Play mode increases loading mass and the boat settles deeper; the character controller itself adds no physical weight. Draft must stay below the full hull height. A separate detailed mesh collider provides a walkable interior, while a convex collider handles physical hull collisions. Water exclusion fits the largest connected hull shell, omitting the rudder, keel and oars. The boundary is capped at 0.85 m and inset 0.025 m inside the planking. The initial waterline is solved from the baked displaced volume.

To test another boat, duplicate the empty scene and place a prepared floating boat prefab. Assign the player's Boat Test Player **Reset Target** and **Reset Local Position** for a return point on its deck; disable **Start Flying** to begin aboard. A bare visual prefab will need its own Rigidbody/buoyancy setup.

Editor tools are under **Island > Boat Tests**. Creation refuses to overwrite existing scenes/prefabs. Validation checks both saved scenes. The Play checks use Unity's start-scene override and restore the previous editing scene setup on exit. Reports go to `/tmp/terranullus-boat-tests.txt` and `/tmp/terranullus-boat-play.txt`.

Use **Island > Boat Tests > Bake Rowing Boat Water Exclusion** to update the existing exclusion asset after hull changes, and **Validate Rowing Boat Water Exclusion** to check the fit. Rebaking preserves its GUID and does not save or regenerate the scene or boat prefab.

**Draft tuning:** select the rowboat's **Boat Physics** object and change **Ocean Buoyancy > Draft** in metres. Hull Draft Loading updates the mass automatically during Play mode. Use its **Apply Draft Loading** context command to update mass in Edit mode. Increasing draft preserves velocity and lets normal buoyancy settle the boat; it does not move the sea or teleport the hull. Changes made during Play mode are temporary. For an older generated prefab, **Island > Boat Tests > Set Up Rowing Boat Draft Loading** installs the component and the 0.45 m default without rebuilding the scene.

**Corrected model trim:** Hull Draft Loading stores the intended resting rotation and calculates displacement at that pose. The rowboat uses a -1.768° pitch; its centre of mass sits below the corresponding centre of buoyancy, with free pitch and roll. Draft changes recalculate both loading mass and balance at the reference pose. After adjusting the boat root rotation in Edit mode, use **Island > Boat Tests > Align Buoyancy To Corrected Rowboat Rotation** to recalibrate the prefab and open instance, including the water probes and initial launch height. It preserves unrelated scene overrides and leaves the scene unsaved. Collider and water-exclusion shapes follow the root rotation without rebaking. **Validate Corrected Rowboat Trim In Current Scene** checks the current unsaved scene against the real ocean, including a temporary 20 cm draft increase, and writes `/tmp/rowing-boat-trim-play.txt`.

**TheOne boat animations:** keep the existing `Locamotion` integer: `0` = walking, `1` = boarding/rowing. Set `PortSide` before requesting entry or exit: true = left/port, false = right/starboard. Set `ClimbFromWater` before entry: false uses the shore step-in, true uses the water climb-up. Each entry finishes once and blends into `Base Layer.Rowing.rowing`; returning `Locamotion` to 0 selects the exit on `PortSide` and finishes in walking. Entry/exit use extracted horizontal and rotational root motion with calibrated seated facing; vertical motion stays in the authored pose. Rowing remains in place and uses `XAxis`/`YAxis` for idle, forward strokes and left/right turns. The live PlayerController supplies these axes from movement input while aboard and disables the walking overlay, standing foot IK, torso look IK and jumping during boat animations while retaining head look. Placeable seat targets drive approach and boarding; Rowboat Controller supplies physical rowing and steering as described below.

**Align TheOne Boat Clip Root Motion** reapplies the six clip import settings, calibrates entry facing against the seated idle on the player avatar, and repairs the existing boat transition paths while retaining the other controller states. **Validate TheOne Boat Animation Paths** exercises both shore and water entries on both sides through rowing and exit, including forward/turning rowing blends; report `/tmp/theone-boat-animation-validation.txt`. **Run TheOne Boat Animation Play Check** checks both shore paths with the real PlayerController and CharacterController on a temporary platform, then restores the current unsaved editing scene; report `/tmp/theone-boat-animation-play.txt`.

**Seats and boarding:** the boat now has `Seats/Rower aft`, `Rower forward`, `Passenger aft` and `Passenger forward`. Move each seat marker to the desired seated hips position and rotate its blue arrow to choose character facing. The initial markers sit 6 cm above the sampled hull seating surfaces. Duplicate a seat to add another occupant position, then set its **Role** to Rower or Passenger. Passenger seats use a quiet seated pose and ignore rowing input.

Each seat generates **Left approach** (green) and **Right approach** (red) children by inverting the actual entry animation's root travel and turn. Left/right are relative to the seat's facing. The points follow the boat and seat transform; their arrows show the character's required entry facing. **Approach Clearance** adds space for the standing capsule. Move approach children for local clearance, or use **Generate Left And Right Approach Points** to restore the calculated positions. Changing **Entry**, **Profile** or **Approach Clearance** regenerates them automatically. **Entry** selects Shore or Water Climb; the latter uses its own measured animation path, while approach movement still uses the current character locomotion.

Press **E** near a free seat: the character reserves the nearest seat and side, walks to its approach marker with normal walking/collisions, aligns to the start pose, then plays entry and settles at the seat. The entry path and seated pose follow the live boat, including pitch and roll. Move, jump or press E again to cancel an approach; blocked or timed-out approaches release their reservation. Press E while seated to exit back to that side's approach marker, facing away, before restoring walking collisions. Inspection/reset also releases the seat. Reservations prevent two characters from targeting the same seat.

`BoatBoarding.TryBoard(seat, fromPort)` lets gameplay or NPC logic request a specific seat and side. `TryBoardNearest()` chooses the closest free approach, `RequestExit(toPort)` chooses an exit side, and `CancelBoarding()` releases a request. `CurrentSeat`, `State` and `BoatSeat.Occupant` expose the result. A rower can drive the existing rowing animation blend. Rowboat Controller connects a passenger to the crew and physical hull; swimming is still separate.

**Set Up Rowboat Seats And Boarding Targets** bakes `RowingBoatBoarding.asset` from the six imported clips and reference player avatar, installs the four seats and the player's Boat Boarding component, and adds the passenger branch to TheOne. It preserves existing seat/approach edits on rerun and leaves the open scene unsaved. **Run Seat Approach And Boarding Play Check** exercises reservations, cancellation, walking before entry, both rowers and a passenger, wave-following seat alignment and exit clearance. It also measures pelvis and hand contact through idle, forward strokes and both turns for each rower. It uses a temporary dock beside the real floating boat, restores the editing scene after Play, and writes `/tmp/boat-boarding-play.txt`.

**Seat and oar IK:** each seat marker is the pelvis target. All four occupants keep their hips on their bench while retaining the animated torso pose. Each rower has left/right **Hand Oar** references; their animated hands swing the corresponding oars around fixed rowlocks, then hand IK holds the grips. Passengers use pelvis IK only. Constraints blend in after boarding and release during exit, leaving the climb animations free to move.

Select a seat to tune **Pelvis IK Weight**, **Hand IK Weight**, **Hand Rotation Weight** and **IK Blend Seconds** (default 0.25 s). Move the **Hand grip** child of each oar model to adjust contact, and rotate it to adjust wrist orientation. The **Oar IK targets** group contains the fixed rowlock markers; move these to change the pivot, and use each **Oar IK Rig > Maximum Swing** to limit its arc. Grip and rowlock gizmos show their relationship. Missing oar references leave that hand animated normally. Rowboat Controller applies the matching propulsion and steering forces to the hull.

**Set Up Rowboat Seat And Oar IK** enables TheOne's base-layer IK pass and installs the four rowlock rigs and grip targets on the wrapper prefab and open instance. It preserves existing valid rig assignments and grip adjustments, leaves the scene unsaved, and does not edit vendor assets. Run it after seat setup when installing into an older test boat.

The forward rower's generated shore approaches need hull-clearance tuning. **Run Forward Rower IK Play Check** raises a temporary dock and approach by 1 m to reach the seat; this tests the actual forward seat/oars without changing the authored approach markers. The aft-rower and passenger cases use their existing markers.

**NPC prefab:** drag `Assets/Prefabs/NPC.prefab` into a scene. It is a variant of Player with the same avatar and `TheOne.controller`, CharacterController, root-motion motor, footsteps, foot IK and Boat Boarding/seat/oar IK. Its shared Player Controller has **Character control > Control Source = External**; it creates no input bindings and never owns the cursor. The player camera, AudioListener and listener-specific shore-wave ambience are removed. NPC footsteps use spatial audio.

The **Npc Controller** starts idle. Assign **Move Target** for an initial destination, or call `MoveTo(worldPosition, running)` / `MoveTo(targetTransform, running)` from AI. Transform targets are followed as they move; `Stop()` clears movement. Steering follows a direct line with normal character collisions; supply waypoints from a navigation system when obstacles require a route. `BoardSeat(seat, fromPort)` walks to a reserved approach and boards using the same animation transitions as the player. `SetRowing(new Vector2(turn, stroke))` drives rower animation/oars, and `ExitBoat(toPort)` leaves on the requested side. `Boarding` exposes the current boarding state and cancellation API. Rowboat Controller takes over crew orders while its command seat has a player, and releases them when the player leaves; NPC rowing commands remain available without a player commanding the boat.

**Island > Characters > Create NPC Prefab** generates the variant when absent and preserves it on rerun. **Run NPC Prefab Play Check** verifies shared assets, independent NPC walking/idle, no NPC input or cursor ownership, boat approach/entry, rowing hand/pelvis IK and exit. It uses temporary platforms and restores the unsaved editing scene after Play; report `/tmp/npc-prefab-play.txt`.

**Explorer deck rowboats:** near either stowed hull on the Explorer deck, press the existing **Use / Interact** key (E by default). The anchor must be deployed; the current helm controls deploy it when sails are furled. The selected deck hull disappears and a floating boat launches on that same side, with two NPC rowers already seated and the player immediately in **Passenger aft**. This skips the shore climb while retaining seat reservations, boat-following poses, pelvis/hand IK and normal exit behaviour. The player's head camera and world streaming follow the transfer. The forward passenger seat remains free. Each stowed hull supplies one boat; repeated use cannot duplicate it.

Launch offsets are editable under **ExplorerShip > Rowboat launch points > Port launch / Starboard launch**. Their X/Z positions use the ship's horizontal heading; height follows the ocean plus the rowboat's authored draft offset. The defaults include clearance for the oars. Select a deck hull's **Ship Rowboat Launch** component to see the spawn bounds and tune **Use Distance**, boat/rower prefabs or passenger seat name. A solid obstruction beside that side rejects the launch and keeps the deck boat and player in place. Crew start in rowing idle with hands on the grips, then respond to the aft passenger’s movement controls.

If the rolling physical ship hull occupies the launch point, **Maximum Hull Clearance Adjustment** permits a short outboard search on the same side (8 m limit by default). This only clears the launching ship; land, other boats and props still reject launch. Physical ship/rowboat hull collisions remain enabled, while their separate moving walking surfaces are excluded from duplicate rigidbody contacts.

**Set Up Explorer Deck Rowboat Launches** installs the two launchers and markers on the existing Explorer prefab and open scene instances, retaining authored hull positions and existing launch-point edits. It leaves scenes unsaved. **Run Explorer Deck Rowboat Launch Play Check** exercises both sides using the actual E action, including anchor-up and obstructed-water rejection, immediate aft seating, two rowers, camera ownership, duplicate-use prevention and floating IK contact; report `/tmp/explorer-rowboat-launch-play.txt`.

**Passenger rowing controls:** while seated in **Passenger aft**, hold **W / Forward** to order both NPC rowers to stroke and propel the boat along its bow (+Z). **A / Left** and **D / Right** steer and select matching turning strokes, including a gentle forward push for a turning stroke from rest. Releasing the controls returns the crew to idle and the hull coasts under the existing water resistance. Leaving the command seat stops orders immediately. The command seat and rower-seat references are editable on **Rowboat Controller**.

Tune **Force Per Rower Newtons**, **Maximum Forward Speed**, **Input Response Seconds**, **Stroke Pulsing**, **Turn Rate Degrees**, **Turn Acceleration Degrees** and **Turn Response Seconds** on the floating boat prefab. Thrust scales with active rower count and fades near the configured through-water speed. Steering uses Rigidbody torque; existing buoyancy, collisions, pitch and roll remain active. Rowers need NPC occupants; the Explorer launch supplies both automatically.

**Fixed oar contacts:** each oar model now has a **Rowlock pin** child. This shaft point is held at the corresponding **Oar IK targets** rig position on the boat through rowing, idle, partial IK blends and release. Move the rig to place the rowlock on the gunwale; move the pin child along the shaft to choose the contact point. The **Hand grip** remains the wrist target. Setup preserves existing seat, grip, pivot and oar rest transforms.

**Blade depth and wrist orientation:** select a rowlock under **Oar IK targets** and adjust **Oar IK Rig > Blade Dip Degrees** (12 degrees by default, 0 for the original animation angle). Positive values pitch the blade down and the handle up around the fixed shaft pin; hands follow the grips. Grip rotations describe wrist-bone orientation and are converted to the avatar's humanoid IK axes at runtime. After editing oar rest rotations or rowlocks, **Calibrate Rowboat Grips And Blade Dip** recalibrates the four grip rotations in the prefab and open rowboat scenes, preserving grip positions, seat positions, oar models and rowlock placement. It leaves scene edits unsaved. **Run Current Crewed Boat Oar Fit Play Check** tests the current crewed scene, including unsaved adjustments, through idle, forward rowing and both turns; report `/tmp/rowboat-oar-fit-play.txt`.

**Exit gravity:** once the exit clip completes, the walking capsule is enabled, the seat reservation/IK are released, and the character inherits the boat’s velocity and returns upright. An unsupported exit falls through open water; the sea shader supplies no walkable collider. Gravity runs once per frame even when no animation motion callback arrives. The CharacterController lands against actual terrain/deck colliders, and the existing foot IK resumes after contact. Animated root height cannot cancel the fall. Cancellation still preserves a deliberately disabled capsule for inspection controls.

**Exit clearance:** the end of the climb moves outboard far enough for the full character capsule, its skin width and **Boat Boarding > Exit Hull Clearance** (0.05 m by default) to clear the boat's walking hull. This avoids balancing the rounded capsule on a gunwale while the visible feet appear to hang over water. The extra distance blends into the exit animation and follows the boat's current roll. Seat markers, approach points and hull colliders retain their authored positions; approach points already farther outboard keep their extra clearance.

**Inspect Exit Support In Current Crewed Scene** tests both sides with the current editing scene and real ocean, then restores the scene and previous Play start setting. It records the player state, actual support object, nearby colliders and collision-ignore flags in `/tmp/boat-exit-current-scene.txt`. **Capture Player Support And Colliders** takes a read-only snapshot at `/tmp/boat-support-main.txt` for inspecting a live hovering character.

**Set Up Rowboat Controls And Rowlock Pins** installs the controller and four shaft contacts without moving authored seats or grips. **Run Rowboat Controls And Exit Gravity Play Check** runs in the standalone ocean template and restores the editing scene afterward. It checks actual W/A/D input, forward motion, both turning blends, idle, fixed contacts, an unsupported exit onto a floor, and walking after landing; report `/tmp/rowboat-controls-play.txt`.
